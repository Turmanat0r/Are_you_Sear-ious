# Recipe notes

Jerk-spiced grilled turkey tenderloin with charred pineapple salsa. Baseline: 1.25 lb turkey tenderloin, serves 4. Grill ambient: 400–425°F at grate level. Use an indirect zone for most of the cook, then a brief direct sear. Start checking at 20 minutes. Remove at 165°F internal; rest 5 minutes. The turkey is poultry, so do not borrow the 145°F endpoint used for whole pork cuts.

Cooking science: lean turkey benefits from even indirect heat and a short sear. Brown sugar and pineapple can flare, so the indirect stage controls the center while the direct stage is brief. A yogurt-free spice paste clings to the surface without hiding thermometer access.
