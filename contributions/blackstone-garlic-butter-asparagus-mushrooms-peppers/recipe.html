Blackstone Garlic-Butter Asparagus, Mushrooms & Peppers

Serves: 4–6 Griddle Temp: About 400°F Cook Time: 10–12 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb asparagus, woody ends trimmed and cut into 2–3 inch pieces
  • 8 oz baby bella mushrooms, thick sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 yellow or orange bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 tbsp avocado oil or olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ¾ tsp coarse black pepper
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • ½ lemon

Blackstone Method

  1. Preheat the Blackstone to medium-high, about 400°F. Add 1 tablespoon of oil.
  2. Add the mushrooms and spread them out. Leave them alone for about 2 minutes so they brown instead of steaming. Flip and cook another minute.
  3. Add the bell peppers and asparagus with the remaining oil. Cook for 4–5 minutes, tossing occasionally, until lightly charred but still slightly crisp.
  4. Push the vegetables together. Add the butter and garlic to an open spot on the griddle. Cook the garlic in the melted butter for 20–30 seconds, then toss everything together.
  5. Season with salt, black pepper, smoked paprika, onion powder and Italian seasoning. Add the Worcestershire sauce and soy sauce and toss for another 1–2 minutes.
  6. Remove from the griddle while the asparagus still has a little bite. Finish with a squeeze of fresh lemon and serve immediately.
Cook's Note: Let the mushrooms develop a good sear before moving them. The Worcestershire and soy sauce add a deep savory flavor without making the vegetables taste like stir-fry.
Pairs Well With: Steak, chicken, pork chops, burgers or grilled fish.
