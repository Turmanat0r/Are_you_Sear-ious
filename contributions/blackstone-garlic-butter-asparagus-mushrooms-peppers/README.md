# Blackstone garlic-butter asparagus, mushrooms & peppers

Supplied by Daniel on 2026-09-12 as a single HTML recipe card, pasted into a
Claude Code session. `recipe.html` is that card exactly as sent. Unlike the
other packages there was no scaffold, no sources file and no image.

## How it was ported

Twenty-fifth cut, second in the Vegetarian category, family `griddle-veg`.

- **Weighed by the whole mix.** The stored weight is the total vegetables,
  2¼ lb for the card's quantities (1 lb asparagus, 8 oz mushrooms, two bell
  peppers), and every line scales from it. 0.45 lb a portion gives the card's
  "serves 4–6" as 5.
- **Salt is measured, and still goes on last.** The card's 1 tsp kosher salt
  becomes the app's 0.5% of weight, 5.1 g, which is about 1 tsp Morton. It
  stays where the card puts it, at the end, and the dry-brine card is hidden
  on this recipe because it teaches the opposite.
- **135°F is not a safety claim.** Vegetables have no USDA minimum. The
  template needs a number, so it uses the FDA Food Code figure for vegetables
  cooked for hot holding, and the copy says that texture decides.
- **Blackstone stays in the title**, with a no-affiliation line in the timing
  note, which appears on the page and on the printed sheet.

## What changed from the card

- `butter` became `unsalted butter`, as everywhere else in the app, so the
  measured salt is the only salt that is not in a sauce.
- The oil is marked `divided` and the steps say half, then the rest, instead
  of "1 tablespoon", so the instruction still holds when the batch is scaled.
- A low-heat holding zone was added, which gives the burner planner something
  real to plan and gives the garlic somewhere to go if the steel runs hot.
- Worcestershire is flagged: most bottles contain anchovy.
- Lemon goes on at the table rather than on the griddle.
- "Pairs well with" moved into the last step.
