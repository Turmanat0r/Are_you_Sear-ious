# Recipe, science and adaptation notes

Repaired 2026-09-10 from the user-supplied `seariously-smothered-beef-ribs-corrected.zip`. Its mustard-bound pepper rub, weighed salt and brown sugar–Worcestershire sauce formula are preserved. The archive's authorship and past test claims are not independently authenticated.

## Primary references

- [Weber / Jamie Purviance: Beer-Braised and Mesquite-Smoked Short Ribs](https://www.weber.com/US/en/recipes/red-meat/beer-braised-and-mesquite-smoked-short-ribs/weber-7841.html): broad braising and sauced-finishing technique. This recipe does not copy its ingredient formula, beer braise or chilling workflow.
- [Weber Grill Academy: Smoked Beef Short Ribs with Sticky Bourbon Glaze](https://www.weber.com/GB/en/recipes/beef/smoked-beef-short-ribs-with-bourbon-glaze/weber-2513232.html): slow indirect cooking, wrapping, and a probe-tender finish around 93°C. This adaptation uses a gas grill and shallow covered pan, not that recipe's charcoal setup or glaze formula.
- [FoodSafety.gov: Safe Minimum Internal Temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures): whole-cut beef 145°F / 63°C with a 3-minute rest; reheated leftovers 165°F / 74°C.
- [FDA: Safe Food Handling](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/safe-food-handling): clean utensils, separation of raw/cooked foods, prompt refrigeration and two-hour/one-hour limits.

The Weber pages and FoodSafety.gov temperature chart were consulted for this repair. Recipe temperatures above the safety minimum, times and seasoning amounts are practical recipe estimates, not government prescriptions or physically kitchen-tested guarantees. Science is explained in original wording, not reproduced publisher passages. No publisher photography is used, and links do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
