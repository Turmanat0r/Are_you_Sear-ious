Choose your burner plan

Light the burners you need and preheat to 425–450°F at grate level. Start direct; keep one cooler edge for flare-ups.

Burners 1–2 lit · use the cooler edge if achiote or honey darkens too fast.

1. Make the achiote marinade

20–30 min

Whisk achiote paste, lime juice and zest, oil, honey, cumin, oregano, garlic, salt, and black pepper. Coat the chicken and refrigerate while the grill heats. Do not marinate overnight in the lime-heavy mixture.

Science: A short acidic marinade brightens the surface. Longer exposure can make the outer layer tight while the center remains unchanged.

2. Grill hot and clean

425–450°F ambient

Follow the grill manufacturer’s lighting sequence, clean the grates, and oil them lightly. Shake off excess marinade so the achiote crust browns instead of burning.

3. Turn once; manage the color

10–16 min estimate

Place thighs smooth-side down. Close the lid and cook 5–8 minutes, turn once, then cook until the thickest piece reaches 165°F. If honey or achiote flares, move to the cooler zone and keep the lid closed.

Science: Thigh meat tolerates high heat better than breast, but the sugar in honey can burn before the center is done. A cooler zone is your control valve.

4. Rest and serve

5 min rest

Move the chicken to a clean tray, rest 5 minutes, then slice across the grain. Serve with charred lime, cilantro, pickled onion, avocado, and warm tortillas.

Safety: Probe sideways into the thickest thigh. Color and clear juices are not substitutes for 165°F.
