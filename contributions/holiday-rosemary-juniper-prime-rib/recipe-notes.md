# Recipe notes

Holiday rosemary-juniper prime rib with horseradish cream. The baseline is a 4 lb bone-in roast; selector options run from 3 to 8 lb. Salt is scaled with size, but cook time is not mechanically scaled: roast geometry and internal temperature decide. Reverse-sear at 250–275°F ambient, then sear over direct heat. Rest 20–30 minutes.

Safety and preference are deliberately separated: USDA lists 145°F with at least a 3-minute rest for beef roasts. A lower serving temperature is a personal preference with higher food-safety risk, not the safety target.
