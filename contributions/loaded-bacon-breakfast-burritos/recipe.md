**Category:** Breakfast / Blackstone Griddle
**Servings:** 6 large burritos
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 20–25 minutes
**Griddle Temperature:** Medium to medium-high, approximately 375–400°F

### Ingredients

* 1 lb bacon, chopped into 1-inch pieces
* 8 large eggs
* 1–1½ cups shredded cheddar or pepper jack cheese
* 1 red bell pepper, diced
* 1 green bell pepper, diced
* 3 cups potatoes, diced into approximately ½-inch cubes
* 6 large burrito-size flour tortillas
* 2 tbsp butter
* 1 tbsp avocado or vegetable oil, if needed
* 1 tsp kosher salt
* 1 tsp coarse black pepper
* 1 tsp smoked paprika
* ½ tsp garlic powder
* ½ tsp onion powder
* ¼ tsp cayenne or chipotle powder, optional
* Salsa or hot sauce, optional

### Instructions

1. **Cook the Bacon:** Preheat the griddle to medium heat. Add the chopped bacon and cook until browned and crisp. Move the bacon to a cooler section of the griddle. Leave some of the bacon grease behind for the potatoes.

2. **Crisp the Potatoes:** Add the diced potatoes to the bacon grease. Season with approximately half of the salt, pepper, smoked paprika, garlic powder and onion powder. Spread into a single layer and cook undisturbed for 3–4 minutes. Flip, add a small splash of water beside the potatoes and cover with a griddle dome. Steam for 3–4 minutes. Remove the dome and cook another 2–3 minutes until browned and crispy.

3. **Cook the Peppers:** Add the diced red and green peppers with a little butter. Cook for 3–4 minutes until lightly charred but still slightly crisp. Mix them with the potatoes.

4. **Scramble the Eggs:** Whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper. Melt 1 tablespoon butter on a medium-low section of the griddle. Pour on the eggs and gently fold with spatulas until soft and just set. Do not overcook.

5. **Warm the Tortillas:** Place each tortilla on the griddle for approximately 15–20 seconds per side until warm and flexible.

6. **Build the Burritos:** Layer crispy potatoes and peppers, bacon, scrambled eggs and shredded cheese down the center of each tortilla. Add salsa or hot sauce if desired. Fold in the sides and roll tightly.

7. **Griddle Toast:** Place each burrito seam-side down on a lightly buttered griddle. Toast for 60–90 seconds per side until the tortilla is golden and crispy and the cheese inside has melted.

### Optional Spicy Butter Finish

Mix:

* 2 tbsp melted butter
* 1 tsp hot sauce
* ½ tsp smoked paprika

Lightly brush the outside of each burrito with the spicy butter before the final griddle toast.

### Cook's Tip

Let the potatoes develop a good crust before flipping them. Cooking them in the rendered bacon grease is what gives these burritos their deep breakfast-griddle flavor. Keep the scrambled eggs slightly soft because they will continue cooking after the burritos are assembled and toasted.
