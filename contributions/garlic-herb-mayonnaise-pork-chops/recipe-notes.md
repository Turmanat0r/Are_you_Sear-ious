# Garlic-herb mayonnaise pork chops

Makes 2–12 servings. Use 1¼–1½-inch bone-in or boneless pork chops.

## Binder

For four chops, mix 1/2 cup mayonnaise, 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce, 2 finely grated garlic cloves, 1 tablespoon chopped rosemary, 1 tablespoon thyme, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika and 1 teaspoon black pepper. Dry-brine the salted chops first, then apply a thin coating. The mayonnaise carries herbs and browns into a savory crust; it is not a heavy sauce layer.

## Grill method

Set up a two-zone three-burner gas grill. Choose the burners yourself: stabilize the direct zone at 450–500°F for searing and the indirect zone at 350–400°F for finishing. Sear 2–3 minutes per side, then move every chop over the OFF zone and close the lid. Begin checking the thickest center around 140°F and remove at 145°F. Rest at least 3 minutes.

Time is a guide, not a doneness test. Thickness, starting temperature, wind and grill behavior control the finish. Reheat leftovers to 165°F and refrigerate within 2 hours.
