# Recipe notes

Chimichurri-orange grilled shrimp with charred citrus. Baseline: 1 lb peeled and deveined jumbo shrimp, serves 4. Grill ambient: 450–475°F at grate level. Use a direct zone for the shrimp and a cooler edge for holding; cook in a single layer and turn once. Start checking at 2 minutes per side. Remove at 145°F internal; opaque flesh and a firm curl are secondary cues, not replacements for the thermometer.

Cooking science: shrimp proteins tighten quickly as heat rises, so a very hot, short cook preserves juiciness better than a long moderate cook. Orange zest perfumes the chimichurri; charred citrus adds bitterness and caramelized sweetness after the shrimp leave the marinade. Do not marinate shrimp in acid for a long time because it changes the surface texture before grilling.
