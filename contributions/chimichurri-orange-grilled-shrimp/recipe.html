Bright heat · Gas grill

Chimichurri.
Orange shrimp.

Fast, garlicky shrimp with charred citrus and a bright herb finish.

GRILL AMBIENT450–475°Fdirect zone, grate level
SHRIMP INTERNAL145°Fthickest shrimp
COOK ESTIMATE4–6 minturn once

Ingredients

1 lb jumbo shrimp, peeled and deveined

1 orange, zested and halved

1 cup parsley and cilantro, finely chopped

2 garlic cloves, grated

3 tbsp olive oil

1 tbsp red-wine vinegar

1 tsp smoked paprika

½ tsp kosher salt

¼ tsp red pepper flakes

Equipment

Instant-read thermometer · skewers or grill basket · tongs · rimmed tray

1. Make the chimichurri

Stir herbs, garlic, olive oil, vinegar, orange zest, paprika, salt, and pepper flakes. Reserve half for serving. Toss shrimp with the other half for no more than 15 minutes while the grill heats.

Science: Keep the acid contact short; vinegar starts changing the shrimp surface before heat does.

2. Light your burners and heat hard

Follow the grill manufacturer’s lighting sequence. Stabilize a grate-level ambient temperature of 450–475°F. Leave a cooler edge or unlit zone for holding. Oil the clean grates.

3. Grill and turn once

Thread shrimp onto skewers or use a grill basket. Cook in one layer for about 2 minutes, turn, and cook 2–4 minutes more. Char the orange halves cut-side down beside them.

Science: Shrimp proteins tighten quickly. High heat plus a short cook builds browning before the center dries.

4. Verify and finish

Probe the thickest shrimp sideways. Remove at 145°F. Toss with reserved chimichurri and squeeze over the charred orange. Refrigerate leftovers promptly.

Safety: Opaque flesh is a clue; the thermometer is the decision.

Source basis: USDA FSIS safe-temperature guidance for fish and shellfish; original flavor and gas-grill adaptation by Are You Sear-ious.
