# Sources and adaptation notes

- USDA FSIS, [Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart): ground meat at 160°F / 71°C; ground poultry, casseroles and leftovers at 165°F / 74°C.
- USDA FSIS, [Grilling Food Safely](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/grilling-food-safely): thermometer use, cross-contamination control and safe grilling temperatures.
- USDA FSIS, [Food Thermometers](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/food-thermometers): thermometer placement and care.
- Weber, [Chiles Rellenos with Tomato Salsa and Guacamole](https://www.weber.com/JP/en/recipes/veggies/chiles-rellenos/weber-4092.html): indirect-medium heat as a stuffed-pepper technique reference.

This is an original Are You Sear-ious filling formula and three-burner gas-grill
method. USDA is the safety authority. Weber informed only the broad indirect-heat
approach and does not endorse this recipe. The 165°F vegetarian setting is a
conservative hot-through target for a dense cooked-rice filling, not a claim that
plain vegetables have the same raw-meat safety minimum.
