# Fire-kissed stuffed bell peppers

Base batch: four large bell peppers, serving about four. Choose halves or upright
cups and scale from 2–12 whole peppers. Batch count changes the ingredient amounts
and required grill space, not the time needed by each pepper.

## Shared base ingredients

- 4 large bell peppers
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cups cooked, cooled long-grain rice
- 1 can fire-roasted diced tomatoes, drained
- 1/2 cup diced yellow onion
- 2 grated garlic cloves
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 6 oz shredded Monterey Jack or pepper Jack
- 2 tbsp cilantro and 1 lime

For vegetarian peppers, add one drained can of black beans and 1 cup corn. For
beef or chicken, add 1 lb ground meat and 1/2 cup corn. The split option uses 1/2
lb beef, 1/2 can beans and 3/4 cup corn, with shared filling divided evenly before
raw meat enters one bowl.

Prepare pepper halves or upright cups. Choose all three burner states yourself,
leaving enough OFF space for every pepper. Hold 375–400°F / 191–204°C at grate
level beside the peppers. Combine the filling and pack it loosely. Keep raw-meat
tools away from vegetarian and serving tools.

Cook covered over the OFF zone. Halves generally need 25–35 minutes; upright cups
need 35–50 minutes. Begin checking at the early end of the range. Probe the
geometric center of multiple peppers. Ground-beef filling must reach 160°F / 71°C;
ground chicken must reach 165°F / 74°C. Vegetarian filling uses 165°F / 74°C as a
conservative hot-through target. For a split batch, identify and check both kinds.
Add the remaining cheese during the final 5–8 minutes.

Rest 5–8 minutes, then finish with lime and cilantro. Refrigerate within 2 hours,
or within 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days
and reheat to 165°F / 74°C. Milk is an allergen; verify that cheese is labeled
vegetarian when that distinction matters. No physical cooking test was performed.
